- **Put the cherub to sleep** from the menu bar: he naps on his pile with z's until you wake him.
- **Light or dark:** a sun/moon button in the headlines popover, or Appearance in the menu bar.
- **A friendlier installer:** the download window shows where to drag the cherub.
- New sources reach your app automatically: Hacker News, The Hollywood Reporter, and recommended sources for every topic; paywalled sources removed.
